intro	Welcome to the Astech Store mobile app, built with React Native and Expo. One codebase ships to iOS, Android and the web, and this tour runs in the browser.
home	The home screen is a searchable catalogue with a category strip on top and a two column grid of products with prices and ratings.
filter	Tapping a category filters the grid in place, and the search box filters on top of it.
product	Tapping a product opens the detail screen, with the photos, the price, the reviews, and a wishlist heart you can toggle.
cart	The cart keeps the line items, the quantity steppers and the total, and it is persisted so it survives a restart.
end	One React Native codebase, running on iOS, on Android, and on the web.
